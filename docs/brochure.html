MyLifeDock

Your family's private life vault. Documents, warranties, insurance, health records, accounts and passwords for everyone at home, encrypted on your own phone or PC.

Download the app

The problem

  • Important papers are spread across drawers, email, WhatsApp and phone galleries.
  • Warranties lapse and policies expire because nobody remembered the date.
  • In an emergency, nobody can find the blood group, policy number or doctor's phone.
  • Cloud apps ask you to trust a company with your family's most private data.

MyLifeDock

  • One organised, searchable place for every record, for every family member.
  • Automatic reminders before warranties, policies, documents and subscriptions expire.
  • An emergency kit per person, ready to print for family or doctors.
  • Encrypted on your own device. No account, no cloud database, no tracking.

Security, in plain words

Only you hold the key to your vault.

AES-256-GCMEvery record and file is encrypted on your device.
Your passphrase is the keyTurned into the key with PBKDF2-SHA-256 (600,000 rounds); never stored or sent.
No account, no serversWe never receive your records, so there is nothing for anyone to leak.
Encrypted backupsGoogle Drive and backup files contain only the encrypted vault.
Auto-lockLocks when idle or when you switch apps; screenshots blocked on Android.
Recovery keyA 64-character key you keep safe, for when the passphrase is forgotten.
