Pricing Guide

Refunds, cancellation and delivery

MyLifeDock Technologies · Effective 26 October 2026

What you buy

MyLifeDock Pro is a digital licence that removes the free vault's 10-item limit in the MyLifeDock app. Plans: Monthly (30 days), Yearly (365 days) and Lifetime. Prices are shown on the pricing page and at checkout, in Indian rupees.

Delivery

  • Website purchases are paid through Razorpay. Your licence key is emailed automatically to the email address you enter at checkout, usually within a few minutes.
  • Nothing is shipped: the product is delivered only by email.
  • If the email has not arrived within one hour (check your spam folder), write to mylifedock@gmail.com with your Razorpay payment ID and we will send the key manually.

Refunds

  • You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of a website purchase by emailing mylifedock@gmail.com with your payment ID. No reason is needed.
  • Duplicate or failed-but-charged payments are refunded in full at any time.
  • Approved refunds are issued through Razorpay to the original payment method. Banks usually show them within 5–7 working days.
  • After a refund, the licence key must no longer be used.

Cancellation

Website plans are one-time payments and never renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel: Monthly and Yearly keys simply expire at the end of their period.

Android and iPhone purchases

Pro bought inside the Android or iPhone app is sold by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Those subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in your Google Play or App Store account, and refunds are handled by Google or Apple under their policies.

Contact

Questions about a payment: mylifedock@gmail.com. See also the Terms of service and the contact page.
