🩺 Medical Profile & Health Records System

Completed: October 22, 2026 | Certified for Android, iOS, Windows Desktop & Web PWA

✨ Executive Summary

MyLifeDock v1.3.0 introduces a comprehensive Medical Profile & Health Records Module. Users can now save, categorize, and filter doctor prescriptions and diagnostic lab reports with date-wise timeline sorting, department tagging with an automatic "General" default, encrypted attachment storage, and local on-device Smart Auto-Fill (Beta) powered by WebAssembly OCR.

🚀 Key Capabilities Delivered

  • Dual Sub-Modules: Separate sub-modules for 💊 Prescriptions & Consultations and 🔬 Lab Reports & Diagnostics.
  • Department Classification: Supports all clinical specialties (Cardiology, Pediatrics, Dermatology, Orthopedics, Ophthalmology, Dental, etc.) with strict fallback to "General" for untagged entries.
  • Date-Wise Timeline: Full chronological sorting (newest/oldest toggles) and instant sub-module tab filtering.
  • ✨ Smart Auto-Fill (Beta): Local Tesseract.js WebAssembly engine parses camera scans and pre-populates dates, departments, clinics, and medication regimens with zero network telemetry.
  • Linked Reminders: Follow-up appointments automatically synchronize with the vault reminders engine.
  • Universal Search: Medical records are indexed and searchable across doctor name, diagnosis, medication, department, or date.
